# propulsion-verification

**Deliverable:** Propulsion verification + oral defense
**Due:** see the course schedule on Canvas

## What belongs here

- Operating-point analysis (code): where your motor's torque-speed curve
  intersects your propeller's load curve, computed for the fixed
  motor-propeller system every student uses.
- Verification that the resulting thrust clears your own frame's
  thrust-to-weight requirement, using your frame's actual measured mass.
  This is individual even though the hardware is fixed: your frame mass is
  your own.
- Defense slides. Log the verification outcome in `../DECISIONS.md`, what
  you checked, what margin you have (or don't), and what would have to
  change if it fell short.
